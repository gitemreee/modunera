# 03 — Risk register (M0)

Date: 2026-10-19. Likelihood and impact are H/M/L judgements against what the
repository shows today; every mitigation names the file or mechanism that
already exists or must be added. Owner abbreviations: **Owner** = business owner
(sign-off, evidence, legal), **Eng** = whoever runs the generators and the
branch, **SEO** = whoever owns the index policy and Search Console.

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **SEO loss on the English home and model routes.** `/en/` and `/en/models/md-N/` are indexable, in the five-language hreflang cluster, and carry the commercial titles/prices introduced 2026-08-25. Repositioning `/en/` to B2B or moving model routes breaks the cluster, the `netlify.toml` 301 map and the x-default chain. | H | H | Keep `/en/` canonical and in its cluster; add B2B routes beside it (e.g. new section), do not retarget existing URLs. Any moved route gets a 301 in `netlify.toml`/`_redirects` in the same commit. Re-run `build-hreflang-v7.mjs --check` and `validate-seo-v7.mjs` before PR. Record current GSC positions for `/en/*` from `data/gsc/` as the baseline. | SEO |
| R2 | **The 14,641 location pages' index gate.** `data/location-index-policy.json` holds 1,561 approved routes; 12,966 are noindex and out of the sitemap. Running any content generator after `build-seo-governance-v7.mjs` re-opens the sitemap to every URL (documented trap). | M | H | Always run the full documented order; CI's "committed HTML equals fresh build" gate catches a partial run only if the branch is built by CI (open a PR — `feat/**` is not in the push list). Never edit the policy's `status` by hand; promotion requires score ≥ 75 or search evidence. | Eng |
| R3 | **Governance collateral.** New B2B pages that are not classified by `build-seo-governance-v7.mjs` are stamped `index` by default (post-2026-08-25 behaviour); drafts or placeholders could enter the sitemap. | M | M | Add new route families to the governance classifier and to `redesign-url-inventory.mjs` `type()`; keep `DEMO_MARKERS` out of copy; hold unfinished pages in `INCOMPLETE_LEGAL`-style noindex lists until sign-off. | Eng |
| R4 | **hreflang clusters.** `build-hreflang-v7.mjs` composes clusters from slug tables and strips sets it did not write; a new B2B section with only EN pages, or EN+DE with different slugs, gets no tag or a wrong one; the validator fails on non-reciprocal sets or links to noindex. | M | M | Register new slug tables in the hreflang builder (or in `data/hreflang-clusters-generated.json` for self-routed generators) before building; a language with no equivalent gets no tag, never the home page. | Eng |
| R5 | **Claims validator vs. B2B copy.** `validate-seo-v7.mjs` fails the build on any `blocked-claims.json` phrase (own production, 13+ years, galvanised, glazing, 220 V, 3,500 kg/CYR & Knott, same-day response, A.TR duty-free), on `aggregateRating`/`review`/`award` in JSON-LD, and on demo markers. The brief's copy will reach for exactly these. | H | M | Write B2B copy against `04-design-and-content-inventory.md` and `11-legal-and-evidence-blockers.md`; add new blocked phrases (and their capitalised / percent-encoded forms) before the first B2B page; delete a rule only when evidence arrives. No testimonials, ratings or awards without named consent. | Owner + Eng |
| R6 | **Unverifiable B2B claims** — turnkey delivery, Africa delivery, certifications (EN 1090, EN 14351, ISO), monthly capacity, engineering capacity for large buildings, reference resorts. None has evidence in the repo; `REQUIRED-BUSINESS-INPUTS.md` §2, §4, §8 are open. Publishing them exposes the site under UWG and to the claims register's own rule. | H | H | Every such sentence is written conditionally or omitted until the owner answers `15-business-owner-inputs.md`; the register gains rules for "turnkey/schlüsselfertig", "ISO", "certified", capacity figures. | Owner |
| R7 | **Form / CRM.** Netlify Forms is the only backend; it was off for four days and lost submissions silently; one field list per form name, posted fields not on it are dropped; notifications go to one mailbox; no CRM, no lead owner, `crmEndpoint` empty. An enterprise proposal form adds fields and expects routing. | H | M | Keep the single form name or register a second form (`projektanfrage-b2b`) with its own field list on a committed page; add fields to all three form generators in one commit; after enabling, push a real change (Netlify detects forms at deploy). Decide lead owner and CRM in `15-business-owner-inputs.md` §11 before launch. | Owner + Eng |
| R8 | **Multilingual quality.** No translation workflow: NL/DA/FR are hand-written JSON paired by index; builders throw on count mismatch; French has sections but no country page; `/sv/` and `/tr/` are stubs. The brief adds languages and B2B terminology (resort, modular community) that the slug tables do not know. | H | M | Start B2B in DE + EN only; add a locale only with the full data set (copy, slugs in `locales.json`, hreflang pairing); native review before index; mark untranslated routes noindex and out of clusters. | Owner + Eng |
| R9 | **Performance.** 15,182 pages rebuilt per run; `assets/images` 20 MB; home shipped 2.16 MB desktop after the August fix; a 45-minute CI budget. Masterplan visuals and video would push weight and LCP. | M | M | Reuse `make_image_derivatives.py` sizing (760/1100/1200/900 siblings) and `build-image-attrs.mjs` lazy loading; no video autoplay; measure with the Chromium pass at 390/768/1440 before merge. | Eng |
| R10 | **Security / secrets.** Public repo; no secrets allowed (`integration-config.json` empty, search key only as Actions secrets, GSC exports are a disclosure decision). Netlify project settings (Forms, notifications, DNS) live outside git. | L | H | Keep every id and key out of the tree; document Netlify settings in `14-deployment-and-rollback.md`; `integration-config.json` written at deploy, not committed. | Eng |
| R11 | **Legal — price advertising (PAngV).** Prices are ex-works indications in `pricing.json`; container price is "delivered to address" on the owner's word; VAT treatment and valid-from dates are open (§3, §5, §6a). B2B pages that quote per-unit or per-project figures without VAT basis and validity breach PAngV/UWG for any consumer-facing page. | M | H | B2B pages quote no price until §5 is answered; where a figure appears, state net/gross, VAT, validity and what is excluded, as the model pages already do. | Owner |
| R12 | **Legal — UWG cold outreach.** The 100-prospect list exists outside the repo; any cold e-mail campaign derived from the site's operator pages needs consent or an existing business relationship under §7 UWG. | M | M | Keep the prospect list in a CRM (README rule); no mailing from site data; outreach policy is an owner decision outside this redesign. | Owner |
| R13 | **Legal — GDPR / KVKK.** Controller, Art. 27 representative, data-flow confirmation and lawyer-approved texts are open (§1). The privacy notice names no entity by rule; Netlify Forms (Netlify, Inc.) and WhatsApp (Meta) are named as processors; no consent-mode analytics id is set. An enterprise form collecting company data in Africa-facing markets adds transfer questions. | H | H | Legal pages stay `noindex` until §1 is satisfied; no new data category in the form without a privacy-notice update in `data/privacy.json`; no analytics id until the cookie flow is reviewed. | Owner |
| R14 | **Legal — Impressum.** `/legal/impressum/` is a placeholder listing full legal name, address, register/MERSİS number, VAT id, representatives, e-mail and EU representative as "noch zu ergänzen". A B2B brand presenting to resorts and municipalities without an Impressum is a credibility and compliance gap. | H | H | §1 of `REQUIRED-BUSINESS-INPUTS.md` is the first owner input; nothing B2B goes live before it. | Owner |
| R15 | **Deployment.** Netlify publishes `main`; `daily-post.yml` and `market-intelligence.yml` commit to `main` every morning; a long-lived feature branch drifts from `main` on 15,000 generated files, and the "committed HTML equals fresh build" gate will fail on a stale merge. | H | M | Branch → PR → Netlify deploy preview → owner sign-off → merge; rebase or merge `main` into the branch before each build; run the pipeline on the branch after every merge from `main`; tag `pre-b2b-redesign-2026-10-19` on `main` before merging (see `14-deployment-and-rollback.md`). | Eng |
| R16 | **Scope creep across the generator layer.** The home and `/faq/`, `/konfigurator/` bodies are legacy-baked and only patched by passes; a hand-edited B2B home would be overwritten by `build-shell-v3.mjs`/`build-modunera-v2.mjs` or fail the idempotency gate. | H | M | Every new page gets a generator or a data file (handoff rule 3); never hand-edit built HTML; update `README-build.md` and all three workflow step lists in the same commit. | Eng |
| R17 | **Brand/visual drift.** The design system is appended to `styles.css` between palette markers and regenerated on every build; `site.webmanifest` still carries old colours; the logo is deliberately unrepainted. New B2B surfaces that bypass `design-system-v2.css` will be inconsistent or lost. | M | L | Extend `tools/design-system-v2.css` only; new tokens in the `:root` block; validator asserts required colours. | Eng |
| R18 | **Evidence drift between documents.** Counts differ across files (15,164 / 15,180 / 15,182 pages; 157 / 535 search-evidence routes) because they were measured on different dates. A redesign plan that cites the wrong baseline misjudges risk. | M | L | Use `02-url-inventory.csv` and `build-report-seo-v7.json` as the M0 baseline; re-run `redesign-url-inventory.mjs` after each milestone. | SEO |

## Open decisions these risks depend on

1. Does `/en/` become the B2B home, or does B2B get its own hub beside it? (R1, R4)
2. Which legal entity signs the Impressum, and when? (R13, R14)
3. Lead owner, mailbox and CRM for enterprise enquiries. (R7)
4. Which markets and languages the first B2B release covers. (R8)
5. Whether any B2B claim in the brief has documentary evidence today. (R5, R6)
