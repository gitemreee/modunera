figure traces: the five things built from `/leistungen/`, the eight plans and
their lengths from `data/pricing.json`, the 2.55 m shared base width from the home

## Pinterest — 497 / 500

```
MODUNERA builds tiny houses, A-frame cabins, modular buildings, bungalows, steel structures and furniture made to measure. Eight plans, MD 1 to MD 8, share one 2.55 m base width, with lengths from 8.00 to 9.70 m. Production is in Türkiye; delivery is to Germany, the Netherlands, Denmark, Luxembourg and Switzerland. Here: floor plans, interiors, façades, production and finished houses — photographs of real units, renders always labelled as concepts. Plans, prices and 110 guides at modunera.com
```

Written differently from the Facebook one on purpose. Pinterest is a search engine
before it is a social network, so the terms people actually type are in the text
rather than implied: *tiny house*, *A-frame cabin*, *modular building*, *floor
plans*, *interiors*, *façades*. The middle sentence says what a visitor will find
on the boards, which is what Pinterest ranks a profile on.

The last clause is the only call to action, and it names what is behind the link
rather than repeating the brand.

## Not in either, and why

- **A founding year or years of experience.** Recorded as missing in
  `REQUIRED-BUSINESS-INPUTS.md`.
- **A price.** `data/pricing.json` has all eight base figures, but the site
  publishes them only with "Indikationen ab Werk; verbindlich ist ausschließlich
  ein geprüftes Angebot." A bio cannot carry that caveat, and a figure without it
  is a worse version of the site.
- **Certifications, standards or partner names.** None is confirmed.

German versions of both can be written from the same facts if the accounts are
run in German — the Facebook page text in `page-text.md` already is.
