## 2. About — the long description

The About tab. Long enough to answer the three questions a stranger arrives with:
what is this, where does it come from, and can I get one where I live.

**Deutsch**

```
MODUNERA fertigt Tiny Houses, Modulbauten, Stahlkonstruktionen, Bungalows und Möbel nach Maß.

Acht Grundrisse stehen zur Wahl, MD 1 bis MD 8, alle auf derselben technischen Basis von 2,55 m Breite; die Längen reichen modellabhängig von 8,00 bis 9,70 m. Rohbau, Hülle, Innenausbau und Möblierung entstehen im selben Haus — deshalb sind Küche, Treppe und Stauraum Teil des Grundrisses und nicht etwas, das nachträglich hineingestellt wird.

Produziert wird in der Türkei. Geliefert wird nach Deutschland, in die Niederlande, nach Dänemark, Luxemburg und in die Schweiz. Ob Straße, Ro-Ro-Fähre oder Tieflader sinnvoll ist, hängt an Zielort, Maßen und Zufahrt und wird projektbezogen bestätigt. Genehmigung und Abnahme bleiben Sache der zuständigen Behörde im Zielland; die Unterlagen dafür liefern wir.

Preise, Grundrisse, technische Angaben und über 100 Ratgeberbeiträge stehen auf modunera.com. Für ein konkretes Projekt genügen Zielland und Grundstück — den Rest klären wir per WhatsApp.
```

**English**

```
MODUNERA builds tiny houses, modular buildings, steel structures, bungalows and furniture made to measure.

Eight plans are available, MD 1 to MD 8, all sharing the same 2.55 m base width, with lengths from 8.00 to 9.70 m depending on the model. Shell, envelope, interior and furniture are made in the same building, which is why the kitchen, the stair and the storage are part of the plan rather than something placed into it afterwards.

Production is in Türkiye. Delivery is to Germany, the Netherlands, Denmark, Luxembourg and Switzerland. Whether road, Ro-Ro ferry or low-loader makes sense depends on the destination, the dimensions and the final access, and is confirmed per project. Permits and sign-off stay with the authority in your country; we supply the documents that go with the application.

Prices, plans, technical details and more than 100 guide articles are on modunera.com. To start a project we need the destination country and the plot — the rest is a WhatsApp conversation.
```

## 4. Not written, and why

Three things a page like this usually claims, left out because nothing here can
evidence them:

- **A founding year or "X years of experience."** Recorded as missing in
  `REQUIRED-BUSINESS-INPUTS.md`. When the legal entity pack arrives it can go in.
- **A price.** `data/pricing.json` carries all eight base figures, but the site
  publishes them only with "Indikationen ab Werk; verbindlich ist ausschließlich
  ein geprüftes Angebot." A bio cannot carry that caveat, and a figure without it
  is a worse version of the site.
- **Certifications, standards or partner names.** None is confirmed. The
  proposed TEMA Vakfı donation is held in `REQUIRED-BUSINESS-INPUTS.md` until it
  can be evidenced and the foundation agrees to be named.

An address is also absent: the workshop's address and the legal relationship to
it are among the inputs still outstanding. Facebook will ask for one — leave the
page as a business without a storefront until that is settled rather than typing
something approximate into a public record.
