# Pinterest — nine pins

German, because Pinterest search is language-specific and the market is German.
Writing these in English would put them in front of nobody who is looking.

**No hashtags.** Pinterest stopped ranking them; the keywords in the title and the
first sentence of the description do the work instead.

**Every pin carries its own link.** This is the one thing Pinterest gives that
Instagram does not — a clickable destination on every post, not just in the bio.
Each goes to the page that answers what the pin raises, not to the home page.

The images are unchanged: the same 1080x1350 files as the Instagram set.

## One that does not belong here

Pin 8 is the fire-safety post. On Instagram it works: it is a feed, a follower
meets it between two other posts, and it says something true without asking for
anything. Pinterest is not a feed. It is a planning surface — people arrive
searching for a house, a plan, a price — and a safety warning ranks for nothing
they are looking for and leads nowhere, because there is no page behind it.

Pin it if you want the board to say what the company cares about. It will not
bring anyone in, and that is not a failure of the post; it is the wrong surface
for it.
