# Triptych

One photograph across three posts. **Publish panel 3 first, then 2, then 1.**
Instagram puts the most recent post at the top left, so posting in reverse is
what makes them land left to right. Pinning does not change the order — pinned
posts move to the front of the grid but keep their order among themselves.

| Post this | File | What it carries |
|---|---|---|
| first | `panel-3.jpg` | the address, bottom right |
| second | `panel-2.jpg` | the slogan |
| third | `panel-1.jpg` | the lockup, top left |

Each panel is a whole post on its own, because in the feed it is seen alone,
days apart from the others. `grid.jpg` is what the profile shows; `strip.jpg`
is the uncut band the three were taken from.

The seams are cut on the square the grid displays, not on the 4:5 post. Cutting
on the post would line the joins up 135 px above where anyone can see them.
