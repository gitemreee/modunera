# Triptych — captions

Three posts, one picture. **Publish 3 first, then 2, then 1.** Instagram puts the
most recent post at the top left, so posting in reverse is what makes them land
left to right. Pinning moves them to the front of the grid but keeps their order
among themselves.

Each is seen alone in the feed, days apart from the others, so each carries a
whole caption rather than a third of one. They are short on purpose: a triptych
is an image, and a long caption under a picture that continues sideways asks the
reader to do two things at once.

The place tag is the same for all three — one photograph, one location field.
Suggested: **Fehmarn** if this goes up before the nine, otherwise leave it off so
the pinned row is not competing with them for the same tag.
