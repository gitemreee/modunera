European location source attribution

The location names and coordinates in europe-locations-source.json are derived
from the GeoNames geographical database via the cities.json package:

- GeoNames: https://www.geonames.org/
- cities.json: https://github.com/lutangar/cities.json
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The MODUNERA build keeps only records for the Netherlands, Denmark, Luxembourg
and Switzerland and adds project-specific slugs and page structure at build time.
