Vlaardingen: housing corporation plans compact housing in the Hoofdstedenbuurt

The Waterweg Wonen project page describes planned compact housing in the Hoofdstedenbuurt. Exact counts and dates are not unambiguously dated on the page and are being re-checked before further publication.

The cited page carries no unambiguous publication date. We therefore state only the date of our own check.

Counts and dates from this source are not yet verified and are deliberately not reproduced here.

What happened

A housing corporation describes plans for compact homes in this neighbourhood. We deliberately do not reproduce counts or dates here: the project page carries no unambiguous date and those figures are not yet verified.

What is clear is the direction: an institutional party is developing compact housing within an existing urban area. That is a different track from a private individual placing a unit on their own plot.

What it means for a buyer

MODUNERA's reading, not a statement by the cited authority.

Corporation projects show that institutional demand for smaller homes exists. They are not a template for a private application, though: a corporation works with its own land holdings, its own programme and its own route through the municipality.

For a private buyer the order stays the same: written confirmation from the municipality about your plot and your intended use first, and only then a model.

Not a permit guarantee

This item reports on a named project in a named place. It says nothing about what is permitted on your plot. The authority responsible for your site decides, in writing, before you order.
