Tiny house permits in Luxembourg: rules & checklist

Updated guidance to tiny-house permission in Luxembourg: site, use, trailer, documents, transport and authority checks.

Short answer

Construction, alteration or demolition of a building in Luxembourg generally requires prior authorisation from the mayor of the municipality. The project must comply with the relevant development and building plans.

1. Use before mobility

Residential, holiday-rental, office or temporary use can lead to different treatment. Actual use and duration usually matter more than the label tiny house, and a trailer is not a blanket exemption.

2. Plot and competent authority

Before ordering, check the address or parcel, intended use, duration, dimensions, utility connections and access with the competent authority in Luxembourg.

3. Technical documents

  • Plans, elevations, sections and dimensions
  • Structure, weights and trailer data where relevant
  • Thermal, moisture, fire and services concepts
  • Placement, foundations, access and unloading

4. Transport is a separate review package

Planning permission at the destination and road legality during delivery are separate questions. Model, route, registration, insurance, driving entitlement and special-transport permits require separate checks.
