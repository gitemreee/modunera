What a tiny house genuinely does better.

Eight advantages that can be tied to figures, schedules and documents – followed by five things a tiny house does not solve. A purchase decision needs both.

Eight advantages

01

A budget you can calculate

Ex-works price, specification line and delivery can be quantified before ordering. What is added locally – foundation, connections, permits – is shown separately instead of disappearing into a lump sum.

02

Short production time

The house is built in the factory while the site is prepared. Both run in parallel rather than in sequence, and weather does not delay production.

03

A small footprint

Less floor area means less sealed ground, less material and in many cases a smaller or cheaper plot.

04

Low energy demand

A compact envelope has little surface through which heat escapes. With insulation, controlled ventilation and an optional solar system, running costs stay manageable.

05

Use can change

The same building works as a home, holiday rental, home office, guest house or office solution. Changing the use is usually a permit question rather than a construction one.

06

It can be relocated

Depending on the build and chassis, the building stays transportable. That keeps options open if the site or your situation changes.

07

One point of responsibility

Steel frame, envelope, interior and furniture come from our own production. Interfaces between trades disappear and accountability stays in one place.

08

A documented basis

Floor plans, elevations, weights, chassis data and specification lists exist. That makes working with authorities, structural engineers and hauliers dependable.

Five things a tiny house does not solve

This list occasionally costs us an enquiry. It also saves both sides a project that fails on site.

Limit

Permits stay site-specific

Wheels or a compact build do not remove planning obligations. Whether a project is admissible is decided by the competent authority for that specific site.

Limit

Floor area is still floor area

Storage, large households and space-hungry hobbies are the real limit. An honest layout check before buying avoids disappointment.

Limit

Transport needs access

Route, turning radii, load capacity and crane standing area all count. A hard-to-reach plot makes delivery noticeably more expensive.

Limit

Plot and financing are separate

Ordering the house is the easier part. Plot, connections and financing follow their own rules per country and municipality.

Limit

Value development is not promised

Rental and resale results depend on location, demand and condition. Figures in example calculations are not a commitment.
