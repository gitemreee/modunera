MODUNERA · Prefabricated facilities

Purpose-built modular facilities for complex sites.

Accommodation, administrative, sanitation and support-building concepts for industrial and institutional developments, structured around practical procurement, access and operational requirements. Each facility type has its own standards; none is presented as a licensed healthcare or education operation.

Scope

Project scope, engineering, permissions and delivery are assessed for each destination in Europe and selected African markets.

Who this is for

Who typically plans this

  • Construction and EPC contractors needing worker camps, site offices and sanitary blocks
  • Mining, energy and infrastructure operators with workforce accommodation on remote sites
  • Public buyers and NGOs planning classroom, clinic and administrative buildings
  • Industrial sites adding security, storage and light support structures

Solution types in this family: Worker camps and workforce accommodation · Administrative and relocatable offices · Classroom buildings · Healthcare facility buildings (the building, not the medical operation) · Sanitary blocks and restroom modules · Dormitory, dining and kitchen modules · Security and control buildings · Storage and light industrial support structures · Steel structure and modular hybrid systems · Emergency accommodation — capacity and tender terms to be verified per project.

Planning and supply

What we can help you plan or supply

  • A facility programme: beds, desks, seats, showers and meals per shift, translated into module counts
  • A campus layout: accommodation blocks, sanitary and dining, administration, circulation, emergency access — schematic
  • A technical submission structure for procurement: drawings, specifications, acceptance criteria, exclusions
  • Climate packages for hot-dry, hot-humid and cold sites, as design axes to be verified
  • Logistics from the hall to the site: road, Ro-Ro, sea container or oversized freight, port, customs, last mile, crane

Site readiness and permissions

What a site needs before a unit arrives

  • Site access for container-size or oversized units, unloading area, crane position
  • Power, water, wastewater and fuel supply, or the off-grid assessment that replaces them
  • Ground preparation and foundations for the facility type
  • The procurement model: private project, public tender, EPC subcontract
  • Local standards for fire safety, accessibility, hygiene and occupancy, verified by local professionals

Climate, energy and infrastructure options

  • Hot-dry: solar gain, shading, thermal performance, dust filtration, passive ventilation
  • Hot-humid: ventilation, condensation, corrosion, coating durability, rainwater management
  • Cold and alpine: insulation, heating, snow load verification
  • Storm, seismic and flood exposure: local engineering analysis, always
