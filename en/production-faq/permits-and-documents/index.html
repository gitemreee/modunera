Dimensioned drawings, elevations, plan, a description of the build-up, details of the services, and the existing evidence for components. What the authority additionally requires — site plan, structural verification for the specific location, drainage plan — comes from the local designer, not the manufacturer.

We assess feasibility from the manufacturer's side: access, crane position, ground, service routes and whether the chosen model can be placed there. Planning and building-law admissibility is assessed by the responsible authority — a different question and a different competence.

Always the body responsible for your site: in Germany the Bauamt, in the Netherlands the municipality via the Omgevingsloket, in Denmark the kommune, in Luxembourg the commune's bourgmestre, in Switzerland the municipality and canton. No manufacturer's statement substitutes for that decision.
