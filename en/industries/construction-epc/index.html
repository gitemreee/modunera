MODUNERA · Industries · Construction and EPC
MODUNERA · Industries · Construction and EPC

Site buildings for construction and EPC contractors.

A project compound — offices, meeting rooms, washrooms, crew housing — delivered in batches to match mobilisation, with the documentation a contractor's QA/QC team asks for.

Send your project briefExplore modular solutions

The buyer's problem

  • Mobilisation dates that do not move
  • Several shipments to one or several sites
  • Temporary buildings that must still meet the client's HSE standard
  • RFQ packages that need a clear scope and exclusions

A modular approach

  • Modules grouped into shipments that follow the mobilisation plan
  • Temporary or permanent specification stated up front
  • Scope and exclusion matrix in every proposal
  • Supply as an EPC subcontract, direct to the client or through a local partner

Project inputs we need

  • Compound programme and staff numbers
  • Mobilisation dates by phase
  • Client HSE and QA/QC requirements
  • Delivery point and Incoterm
  • Installation responsibility
Limits

QA/QC documents, inspection and test plans are agreed per contract. No standard certificate is claimed.

Building types

Relevant building types

MOD-OF · EPC offices, meeting, security, washrooms

Site offices and sanitary

Offices, meeting rooms, security cabins and washrooms for a construction or EPC site — relocatable when the project moves on, or specified for permanent use..

MOD-FP · Offices, dormitories, sanitary modules

Flat-pack modular units

Container-type modules shipped as compact packs and assembled on site: offices, dormitories, sanitary and storage units.

MOD-WC · Accommodation for remote project sites

Workforce camps

Modular accommodation, sanitation, dining and administration concepts structured around workforce capacity, site access and phased delivery.

Process

From brief to installation

Delivery terms (EXW, FCA, FOB, CIF, DAP or other) are agreed per project and per Incoterms edition in the contract. Nothing on this page fixes a delivery term, a price or a lead time.

  1. BriefYour programme, site, country, quantities and timeline.
  2. FeasibilityExport, customs, permits, transport and partner questions for the destination.
  3. Preliminary layoutModule types, counts and a schematic site arrangement.
  4. ProposalScope, exclusions, delivery terms and the open points.
  5. Engineering approvalStructure, building physics and services against the applicable local codes, by qualified engineers.
  6. ProductionBuilt to MODUNERA's specification once drawings and specification are signed off.
  7. Quality checksInspection points agreed in the contract.
  8. ShipmentRoad, Ro-Ro or sea freight to the agreed delivery point.
  9. InstallationBy MODUNERA's team, a local partner or the client's contractor — as the contract sets out.
