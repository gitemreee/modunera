The plan

How MD 3 is laid out.

The extra room sits at one end and is separated from the living area by a door. That is the decisive difference from an open plan: a video call, a sleeping child or a guest no longer occupies the whole house. In the 9.70 metre version the living area stays generous despite the room.

Particularly suited to

  • Home office with video calls
  • Couples with occasional guests
  • Families with one small child

Technical basis

What MD 3 is built from.

The exact execution is fixed in the order and production specification. The points below are the basis that a project then builds on.

Load-bearing steel structure

The load-bearing structure is steel, not a timber stud frame. Which corrosion protection process is used, and to which standard, is stated per model in the technical data sheet attached to the quotation — it is an order specification, not a blanket product property.

Steel chassis

The chassis is designed as part of the building, not bolted on as an accessory. Axle load, centre of gravity and lashing points all shape how the house survives the road into Central Europe.

Mass and road classification

Axle, braking and frame are specified per model. The actual mass of the delivered configuration is weighed before dispatch and confirmed by a weighbridge ticket. Whether a unit counts as a vehicle or a building in the destination country is decided by the authority there — width alone does not make a finished house road legal.

Thermally broken windows and doors

Windows and doors are thermally broken. In Denmark and Switzerland the glazed area is often the single largest heat loss, so it is sized for the site rather than by a general rule.

Insulating glazing, specified per model

The glass build-up is fixed per model. Ug and Uw values and the manufacturer's declaration are attached to the quotation. On panorama surfaces the summer solar gain matters as much as the U-value, so the two are considered together.

Halogen-free installation for 230 V / 50 Hz

The electrical installation is halogen-free and designed for 230 V / 50 Hz. Connection, protection and testing in the destination country are carried out by a locally licensed electrician to the standard that applies there.

Specification

What gets decided per project.

These are not fixed in advance. Site climate, how the house will actually be used and the budget determine the sensible answer.

Façade

Thermowood+, composite panel or standing seam. The choice follows climate and appetite for maintenance, not only appearance — standing seam asks less of you in snow country, Thermowood+ changes visibly and deliberately.

Insulation

The build-up is specified for the heating season at the destination. A house for the Upper Rhine and a house for Jutland are not the same house, even with an identical plan.

Heating

Heat pump, underfloor heating or a combination. What decides it is real occupancy: a permanently lived-in house and a holiday unit on frost protection need different systems.

Ventilation

Mechanical ventilation with heat recovery is the most effective moisture control in a compact volume. Without it, user behaviour decides the moisture content of the structure.

Solar and storage

Photovoltaics with battery storage can be prepared for or planned in full. Consumption profile, roof area and winter yield at the site decide it — not module rating alone.

Built-in furniture

Kitchen, bathroom, storage and stair come from our own joinery. Dimensions are therefore not limited to catalogue modules but matched to the actual plan.

Climate

MD 3 across the five markets.

The same plan behaves differently in Zurich than in Aalborg. These points are fixed before production, not retrofitted afterwards.

Germany

Germany runs from the mild Upper Rhine to the Alps and the Baltic coast. Heating season, snow load zone and wind zone at the actual address govern. For permanent living the build-up is usually one step heavier than for holiday use.

Netherlands

The Netherlands is mild but damp and wind-exposed. The emphasis is less on insulation thickness than on airtightness, moisture control and ventilation. Near the coast, salt load on the façade is added to that.

Permits

What has to be checked locally.

MODUNERA delivers a building, not a permit. Whether MD 3 may be placed and used at a specific address is decided solely by the authority responsible there. The notes below do not replace that check — they tell you what to ask.

Permits

There is no blanket permissibility for tiny houses. The local plan, land-use designation, servicing and the intended type of use decide each case. Ask the municipality before you buy — in writing.

Germany

The lower building authority of the district or independent city is responsible. Clarify the Bebauungsplan or §34/§35 BauGB, the permissible use, servicing, and whether a Baugenehmigung or an exemption procedure applies. Building codes are state law and differ.

Official source →

Netherlands

Applications run through the Omgevingsloket. Clarify the municipal omgevingsplan, whether an omgevingsvergunning is required, and whether the site is designated residential, leisure or recreational. Recreatiewoningen carry their own limits on use.

Official source →

Denmark

The kommune is responsible. Clarify the lokalplan, the zone status, and whether a byggetilladelse under the Bygningsreglement is required. In sommerhusområder, year-round residential use is generally restricted.

Official source →

Luxembourg

The commune is responsible. Clarify the PAG and PAP, admissibility in the relevant zone, and the autorisation de construire. Because municipalities are small, practice differs noticeably from place to place.

Official source →

Switzerland

Municipality and canton are both involved. Clarify the building zone, the land-use plan and the Baubewilligung. Outside the building zone, approval is possible only in narrowly defined exceptions — the most common reason Swiss projects fail.

In use

After handover.

A compact building forgives neglect less readily than a large one. Moisture that would go unnoticed in 140 square metres becomes visible in 28 within a single heating season. Ventilation — whether mechanical or manually disciplined — is therefore the most important operational point, not heating.

The façade sets the maintenance rhythm. Thermowood+ greys in a controlled way and mostly needs looking at; coated composite panels and standing seam ask less but require clean junctions and free drainage. Roof, gutters and the transitions around windows deserve one proper inspection a year.

Renting changes the arithmetic. Cleaning between stays, laundry, wear on hardware and surfaces, and reserves for replacement are the real cost drivers — not energy. Budgeting for them up front is what keeps the return where you projected it.

Questions

Common questions about MD 3.

MD 3 is laid out for 2–4 sleeping places. For permanent living we suggest planning at the lower figure; the upper figure is realistic for guests and short stays.

MD 3 is planned in 8.00 m or 9.70 m. All models share a 2.55 metre base width, which keeps transport on European roads within normal dimensions.

The build-up is specified for the heating season at the destination: insulation thickness, glazed area, ventilation and heating system are decided together. A house for year-round living in Denmark is specified differently from a summer unit on Lake Constance.

That is decided by the authority at the site, not by the manufacturer. Local plan, type of use and servicing determine the procedure. Settle it in writing with the municipality before ordering — we supply the technical documentation for it.

Production time is scheduled at order confirmation and depends on the model, specification depth and workshop load. Transport to central Europe is added to that; the exact date is fixed together with the route and unloading point.

Delivery is by low-loader to the agreed point. Unloading by crane or heavy-duty forklift is clarified in advance from access photographs, dimensions and ground conditions. Narrow or steep access is solvable, but has to be known early.
